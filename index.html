🎯 Purpose
Skejo is a productivity tool designed to eliminate procrastination and empower users to plan their days with intention. By building structured habits and routines — called Routines — Skejo helps users take consistent, meaningful action toward their goals.
⚙️ How it Works
1
Register or log in to your Skejo account.
2
Create your first Routine — a personal habit or daily routine you want to build.
3
Track your progress and stay accountable every day.
🌐 Platform Availability
Skejo is primarily a web-based application accessible from any modern browser. Users can also install it as a native app on Windows or mobile devices:
Navigate to your Profile (top-right avatar) → tap Install App to download the Windows or mobile version.

Install Skejo
Access Skejo from your home screen offline
🍏 iOS Safari Installation Steps
1
Tap the Share button ⎋ (or 📤) in Safari's bottom toolbar.
2
Scroll down the share menu and select "Add to Home Screen" ➕.
3
Tap "Add" in the top-right corner to complete the installation.
🤖 Android / Desktop Installation
1
Tap the browser's **menu** button (usually three dots ⋮ in the top-right).
2
Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" from the list.
🚀 Why Install? Installing creates a direct app icon alongside your other apps, removes browser toolbars, enables full offline support, and ensures alarms trigger reliably.
